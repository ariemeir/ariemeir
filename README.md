### [RoboImplant](https://github.com/ariemeir/roboimplant) · non-invasive spinal implant adjustment system

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ariemeir/roboimplant/main/docs/images/driver-unit-assembled.jpg" width="560"
       alt="The assembled handheld driver unit in a 3D-printed orange enclosure with an acrylic top plate.">
</p>

A motorized orthopedic implant that lengthens a spinal distraction rod without
surgery, for treating early-onset scoliosis in growing children. AVR C firmware
on an ATmega1284P drives a maxon ESCON servo controller through a DS1267 digital
pot, with current-threshold coupling detection as the primary safety interlock.
An Android clinician console talks to it over Bluetooth SPP using a custom
line-oriented ASCII protocol, with live telemetry, 3D visualization and patient
records. The controller board and its analog sensing chain were laid out in
EAGLE; the handheld enclosure in SolidWorks.

Embedded · Mobile · Electronics · Mechanical. Research prototype, ~2012 to 2013.
Not a cleared medical device.

### [3D Scanner](https://github.com/ariemeir/3dscanner) · turntable photogrammetry rig

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ariemeir/3dscanner/main/docs/images/original-and-print.jpg" width="560"
       alt="A white ceramic daisy dish beside a dark grey 3D-printed copy of the same flower.">
</p>

A DIY tabletop scanner built to solve a real fabrication problem: an artist needed
larger and deeper variants of a commercially molded clay flower that existed only
as a physical object. It takes that object and produces a printable, watertight
STL. An iPhone runs **ScannerCam**, a camera server with a hand-rolled HTTP/1.1
stack on `NWListener` and hard locks on focus, exposure and white balance. A
Python controller drives the scan loop across two machines with idempotent
retries, resumable sessions and a five-stage SHA-256 integrity chain. An Arduino
turns the turntable by impersonating its infrared remote, replaying NEC codes
reverse-engineered with a majority-vote capture sketch.

The engineering centrepiece is control of an actuator that cannot be observed:
the turntable answers only to a fire-and-forget infrared toggle, so the controller
tracks an explicit assumed state and halts for human realignment on any ambiguity
rather than guessing and silently desynchronising the scan.

iOS · Orchestration · Embedded · Reconstruction · Print prep. Personal project, 2026.

### [WifeSignal](https://github.com/ariemeir/wifesignal) · a traffic light for the office door

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ariemeir/wifesignal/main/docs/images/signal-lit-green-on-desk.jpg" width="560"
       alt="A small black 3D-printed traffic light on a desk, its bottom green lens glowing and casting green light across the surface.">
</p>

A three-colour desk lamp that lets one person in the house tell another how much
this can wait. The office is a separate building: music on, windows closed, phone
face-down for hours, which is the point and which makes it a sealed box. The
missing piece was never notification, it was priority expressed before the
interruption happens. A SwiftUI app in 333 lines sends the priority; a 290-line
aiohttp and bleak bridge on a Mac carries it over BLE to ESP32-C3 firmware; a
custom two-layer KiCad board lights one of three illuminated arcade buttons.
Pressing that button acknowledges the message and the sender's phone shows
**Seen ♥**. The enclosure is generated by a 440-line Fusion 360 script.

The engineering centrepiece is the return path. Three different events, the
server's own write echoed back, a human pressing the button, and a device that
has drifted, all arrive as an identical one-byte notification. They are separated
with no timers, correlation IDs or sequence numbers: every notify is compared
against the state the server already intends, which collapses the problem into
three branches and makes recovery after a reboot or a reflash automatic.

iOS · Server · Embedded · Electronics · Mechanical. Personal project, 2026. In daily use.
